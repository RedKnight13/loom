Responsibility stays visible

Three roles.
Different authority.

Owner
Sets the intent and constraints, resolves decisions, and controls what ships.
Conductor
Writes the mandate, assigns the worker, checks the result, and handles GitHub publication under the owner’s authority.
Worker
Works within the assignment and reports facts. Does not push or take over another ticket.

Four reports. Four next steps.

The conductor reads structured state. It does not guess completion from a confident paragraph.

ready
A reviewable result exists.
Run the checks and inspect the work.
blocked
A decision is needed.
Answer the concrete question or take it to the owner.
failed
The approach failed.
Read what happened before deciding how to continue.
waiting
The turn is done; work may continue.
Leave the assignment for its next authorized step.

Evidence has limits

A passing test tells you what that test checked. A separate workspace does not replace a security sandbox. Loom keeps the scope, reports and verification explicit; it cannot make an incomplete check prove correctness.
