## Defects found while drilling (fixed before certification)

1. **Watchdog was global.** A 1-minute drill run would also have killed the
   live D1/D3 codex workers. Caught before running; fixed with
   `watchdog.sh --id`, covered in selftest (out-of-scope worker survives).
2. **A hand-run kill orphans children.** Retiring the pre-loom pyz
   worker with a plain `kill <pid>` left its `pytest` (pid 22795) running,
   reparented to init. This is the failure v1's process-group kill exists
   for; D2 shows the v1 path leaves nothing behind.
3. **Ticket/template drift.** the consumer#42 was written as `## Mandate (loom)`;
   dispatch refused it ("issue has no Mandate block"). The ticket was fixed
   to the template, not the parser loosened: the template is the law.
